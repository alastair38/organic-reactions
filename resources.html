Exam craft

What the markers want

Organic questions reward precise functional-group language, correct structures and a clear link between observation and explanation.

Command words

  • State / name: one word or short phrase; no explanation needed.
  • Describe: say what you see or what happens, including colour changes and products.
  • Explain: give the reason, using bonding, functional groups or reaction type.
  • Draw: show full or shortened structural formulae, with correct bonds and labels.
  • Compare: give both sides, e.g. fats vs oils, denaturation vs hydrolysis.

One-page revision recipe

  • Reaction map: 8 core transformations with arrows and reagents.
  • Observation bank: orange → green; black → brown; brick-red; silver mirror; decolourised.
  • Structure bank: ethanol, ethanoic acid, ethyl ethanoate, glycerol, soap ion, peptide link.
  • Explanation bank: hydrogen bonding, London dispersion forces, hydrolysis, condensation, radical stages.
  • Error log: every missed mark, rewritten as a one-line rule.
