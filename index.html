At a glance

Core reactions to know

If you can complete this table from memory, you have the skeleton of the organic chemistry unit.

Reaction Functional group change Key reagent / condition Tell-tale sign or product
Oxidation of a primary alcohol –OH → –CHO → –COOH Acidified dichromate(VI), heat; excess oxidant for the acid Orange → green; aldehyde then carboxylic acid
Oxidation of a secondary alcohol –OH → C=O (ketone) Acidified dichromate(VI), heat Orange → green; ketone; no further oxidation
Aldehyde tests –CHO → –COOH Fehling's, Tollens' or acidified dichromate(VI) Brick-red precipitate; silver mirror; orange → green
Esterification –COOH + –OH → –COO– Concentrated sulfuric acid catalyst, warm Ester + water; fruity smell; reversible
Ester hydrolysis –COO– → –COOH + –OH Water with acid, or aqueous alkali Carboxylic acid + alcohol, or carboxylate salt + alcohol
Bromine test for unsaturation C=C → C–C Bromine solution, shake Orange-brown → colourless
Soap formation Triglyceride → glycerol + soap NaOH, heat (alkaline hydrolysis) Glycerol + sodium salts of fatty acids
Protein condensation / hydrolysis –NH₂ + –COOH ⇌ –CONH– Condensation; hydrolysis with water/enzymes Peptide link + water; amino acids on hydrolysis
Free radical chain Bond homolysis → radicals UV light or initiator; propagation; termination Radical equations; stable product at termination

How to revise

Use the 20-minute loop

Short, active sessions beat long passive reading. Each loop ends with evidence that you can retrieve the chemistry without notes.

01

Close the book

Write the reaction map, definitions or equation from memory. Retrieval is the learning, not the test after learning.

02

Draw it

Use full or shortened structural formulae. Label the functional group, reagent, condition and observation. A diagram you can draw is a concept you own.

03

Apply it

Answer one exam-style question and mark it against the scheme. Add the mark you missed to an error log; revisit it in three days.

Retrieval

40 flashcards →

Flip, shuffle, filter by topic and mark cards for review. Your known/review piles are saved.

Check

Quiz arena →

Multiple choice, true/false, fill-the-gap, matching, equation completion and exam-style answers.
